PROTOTYPE // FOOTER-MEGA-V3

the footer, transmitting.

Third pass on .fm2. Upgrades over v2: a signal sweep — a dithered brightness band that crosses the wordmark every ~8s on top of the per-stroke flicker (a masked echo layer, hand-set alignment untouched); meteors — a short ·:* streak crossing the starfield every ~15s; a real instrument readout status strip (led, ver, audit, loc, relay, ticking uptime, flagged [ demo ]); and bracket hover links whose [ ] slide outward while the // desc brightens. All motion sits behind IntersectionObserver + visibilitychange + prefers-reduced-motion. Copy stays honest: pre-1.0, not externally audited.
