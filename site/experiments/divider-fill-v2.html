dividers that fill like blocks

Replacement for the plain hairline between sections. Each .dvf rule is real mono text — selectable and printable — that sweeps left→right through the ordered ramp ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█ when it enters the viewport, then settles static. Leave and return: it re-arms and fills again. About every 12s one settled block flashes ember and cools.
