what actually crosses the wire. peers handshake with noise_xx end-to-end; the relay in the middle is a blind courier — it routes opaque frames, keeps bounded mailboxes for offline peers, and never holds a key.

++ ++

Topology diagram: YOU, peer-id 4d2e…f18c, runs a Noise_XX x25519 handshake to the blind relay, which routes opaque ciphertext onward to PEER, peer-id 9c1f…aa07. The relay sees only timing and size, nothing else. A mailbox queue mbx·7e3d hangs off the relay holding frames for offline peers. Peers pin each other's static key and meet through one-time cinder:// invite links.
