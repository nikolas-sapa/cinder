E2E-ENCRYPTED TERMINAL MESSENGER

The wire is yours.
Nothing else is.

cinder is a Rust terminal messenger with end-to-end encryption built in from the first packet. No servers reading your traffic, no webviews, no telemetry — just a socket, a keypair, and a TUI that stays out of the way.

LEGIBILITY

Text over the frame

The dither lives on a fixed layer under all content. Body copy, code blocks, and panels remain fully readable — the dots only occupy the outer 60–90px of the viewport, and this demo page keeps a normal content measure inside that zone.

Handshake in one line. Peers exchange Noise XK handshakes over TCP, upgrade to a ratcheted channel, and render the session as a single TUI surface. Contact discovery is explicit: you share a fingerprint, not a phone number.

// ~/.config/cinder/peers.toml
[peer.fern]
fingerprint = "9f2c:71ab:…:e40d"
onion       = "fern…7d.onion:7823"
ratchet     = "double"

[ui]
theme = "ember"
frame = "dither"   # what you're looking at

Full-bleed check. This band deliberately lets body text run across the left and right dither strips to prove legibility at the edge. The Bayer-ordered dots thin out fast enough that the first characters of each line sit on a nearly clean ground, while the printed-decay texture is still visible in the margin.

Density falls off inward. At the extreme edge roughly nine of ten cells are lit; by ~40px in, the threshold matrix passes fewer than half; past the strip boundary nothing draws at all. Corners take the maximum of both falloffs, so they stay dense without a doubled pattern.

Two fixed-size viewports render the same paint routine inline. The 1440px panel scrolls horizontally if your window is narrower — the dither strips stay a constant 76px regardless of panel width.
