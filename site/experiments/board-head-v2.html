what actually crosses the wire. peers handshake with noise_xx end-to-end; the relay in the middle is a blind courier: it routes opaque frames, keeps bounded mailboxes for offline peers, and never holds a key. press v or the view chip to see what the relay sees.

++ ++

Topology diagram with two switchable views. Transport view: YOU, peer-id 4d2e…f18c, runs a Noise_XX x25519 handshake to the blind relay, which routes opaque ciphertext onward to PEER, peer-id 9c1f…aa07. Relay view (press v): the same path as the relay sees it: anonymous connections, frame sizes and timing deltas only; all payloads render as unreadable dithered blocks. The relay keeps bounded mailboxes for offline peers and stores no keys. Status readouts are demo values.
